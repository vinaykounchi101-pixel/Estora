# Estora — Design System 3: Architectural Graphite & Champagne Bronze

Version: 1.0 (Design System 3 Specification)
Location: `docs/design-system/design_tokens.md`
Concept: Ultra-Luxury Private Wealth & Architectural Studio

---

## 1. Design Direction
An avant-garde private client real-estate advisory aesthetic. It combines cool architectural alabaster stone surfaces with deep graphite structuring and warm champagne bronze data accents.

---

## 2. Color Tokens

### Core Palette
- `canvas`: `#F5F4EF` — Alabaster stone page canvas
- `surface`: `#FCFCFA` — Elevated crisp white stone cards
- `surface-elevated`: `#EDEAE1` — Secondary panels and subtle wells
- `graphite`: `#181B1E` — Deep architectural structural headers and dark panels
- `graphite-soft`: `#5C646C` — Supporting subtext and descriptive copy
- `champagne`: `#C5A880` — Subtle warm champagne metallic accents & active highlights
- `forest`: `#2D3A34` — Deep architectural pine green for success & verified status
- `terracotta`: `#9E4738` — Subdued earth red for warnings and negative trends
- `border`: `#DCD8CD` — Thin sandstone hairline dividers

---

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Display:** `Cormorant Garamond` / `Cinzel` (Editorial luxury authority)
- **Body & Controls:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Geist` (Modern high-clarity typography)
- **Financial Figures:** Tabular lining numerals

---

## 4. Spacing, Borders & Elevation
- `radius-sm`: `4px` (Sharp, tailored controls)
- `radius-md`: `8px` (Inputs, buttons)
- `radius-lg`: `14px` (Feature cards and valuation panels)
- `radius-pill`: `999px` (Status badges)
- `shadow-subtle`: `0 4px 20px rgba(24, 27, 30, 0.05)`
